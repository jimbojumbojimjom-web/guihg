Tips

Headphones recommended. The exit hum is faint. Most doors are fake, so find a map.
Brown is fast and his face creeps over your screen. Green always knows where you are. White walks through walls and fills your head with static. They arrive one at a time. Every hit jumpscares you and costs at least 50 HP: brown 50, white 50, green 75 on Normal difficulty (see Settings). The exit is very well hidden and most doors are fake, so look for a map. Also look for medkits and a flashlight. Coins are lying around: collect them to unlock stuff in the Shop. The real exit is the wooden door: find a hammer, then hit it three times (E or click). Some walls are fake and shimmer: walk straight through them. Dying costs 15 coins, escaping pays 50.
